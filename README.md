# PRODUCING-THE-FZD7-CRD-RECOMBINANT-PROTEIN-TO-INHIBIT-THE-WNT-SIGNALS-AND-CSCS
Abstract
(EN) The present invention focuses on creating a soluble FZD7 receptor as a potential treatment for cancer. This new method aims to inhibit the Wnt/[3-catenin signaling pathway, which is crucial in controlling cancer cell behaviors like growth and survival, especially in cancer stem cells. CSCs are difficult to treat due to their ability to spread, return, and resist common treatments, so blocking this signaling pathway could effectively target these tough cells. The soluble FZD7 receptor, produced through recombinant technology, binds to Wnt ligands that typically activate the Wnt/[β-catenin pathway. By preventing these ligands from linking to FZD7 cell surface receptors, the soluble receptor can hinder Wnt signaling, thus reducing the formation of CSCs. This invention can be applied to treat various cancers, including breast, gastrointestinal, lung, and skin cancers, potentially improving treatment effectiveness and minimizing side effects. This method represents a promising advance in targeted cancer therapies.

Ce procédé représente une avancée prometteuse dans des thérapies anticancéreuses ciblées.
